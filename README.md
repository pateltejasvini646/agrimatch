# 🌾 AgriMatch – Smart Farmer Scheme Finder

**Connecting farmers with the right government schemes through technology.**

## 📌 About the Project

AgriMatch is a full-stack web application designed to help farmers discover government agricultural schemes based on their individual requirements, including state, crop type, landholding and farming category.

The platform simplifies the process of finding suitable government schemes by providing relevant information about eligibility, benefits, required documents and official application portals in one place.

## ✨ Key Features

- **State-wise Scheme Matching:** Discover agricultural schemes based on the selected state.
- **Personalized Recommendations:** Find potentially suitable schemes using farmer details and requirements.
- **Crop-Based Filtering:** Explore schemes relevant to different crop types.
- **Eligibility Information:** Understand scheme eligibility criteria and benefits.
- **Document Requirements:** Access information about documents needed for applications.
- **Official Portal Links:** Navigate to government portals for further details.
- **Search & Filter:** Easily explore and find relevant schemes.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. Farmers enter their basic details.
2. Select their state, farming category, crop and landholding.
3. Choose their required type of agricultural support.
4. The system processes the information and matches relevant schemes.
5. Farmers can explore scheme details, benefits and eligibility.
6. Official portal links help farmers access further information.

## 🎯 Project Objective

Many farmers are unaware of government schemes that could support their agricultural activities. AgriMatch aims to reduce this information gap by bringing relevant scheme information together through an easy-to-use digital platform.
